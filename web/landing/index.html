What's inside

One organism, nine species

Each engine is a species, drawn by the firmware itself. On HOME every track is a creature of its engine's species: its shape comes from its knobs, and it swells and heats up with its own audio.

The nine engines, as the FM-1 draws them

Aengines

11 synthesis engines

ANALOG, FM6 (six operators, 32 algorithms, DX7 banks), PHASE, LOFI, VOICE (it sings words in Spanish, English and Japanese), WHEEL, NOISE, ANIMAL (birds, insects and frogs from physical models), STRING (a plucked, struck or bowed string), ADD (additive) and DRUM-X. No samples: everything is synthesized.

Bheart

DRUM-X

A drum kit of eight synthesized sounds, each with two sides: MORPH moves the whole kit from one to the other, live. In every octave the same keys: ratchets above, short, ghost and flam hits below.

Cnervous system

64-step sequencer

A piano roll per track, chords, probability, ratchets, extra playheads, recording off the grid to a 64th of a step, and MUTATE, which transforms sound and pattern every few bars.

Dmembrane

Effects and master

Delay, five reverbs (SHIMMER, RESO, CLOUD…), chorus, slicer, live punch-in effects, a DJ filter on the whole mix and a master with limiter, clip, punch, tape and a 4-band EQ.

Ememory

Songs and the generator

Four sections per project, an ARRANGE page to chain them, and a generator that writes a whole song idea: from a genre, or in EXPLORE, breaking every rule.

Froots

USB audio and MIDI

The FM-1 becomes an audio interface that keeps its time: every track on its own channel into your DAW (STEMS), ready to record each part on its own. MIDI over USB, Bluetooth and TRS.

Find your way

Six rules, one map

Since 0.9.2 every section works the same way: learn six rules once and the whole synth is known. The map shows each button's groups and pages in the order SELECT walks them, in any of the three skins (AIR, COLORS, HUD).

1SELECT

SELECT walks the pages

One chain per section, a page a detent; it stops at the first and the last page.

2button

The button: in, or on

From another section, the page you left there. Inside, the first page of the next group, round.

3PRESETS

PRESETS loads presets

The track's sounds everywhere, the patterns in SEQ, the genre in GEN. Never a page or a track.

4KNOB 1

KNOB 1 picks

The step, the bar, the slot, the stage, the kit's sound. The track is always ALGORITHM's.

5hold

Hold = the layer

A button held puts its shortcuts on the keys: SEQ + G5 clears a step, SAVE + C5 names, GLO + G4 undoes. Held + SELECT: GEN, the dice, the tempo.

6screen

The screen says where

Tabs on top are the section's groups, a dot a page; the footer says SECTION · GROUP · page and n/N.

FAQ

Before you install

Can I go back to the official firmware?

Yes. The installer has a button to return to the official V15 firmware (you choose the official FM-1.fwsc from M-VAVE). It saves a complete backup first.

Which browser do I need?

Chrome or Edge on a computer, for the installer and the editor: they talk to the FM-1 over USB. The emulator also works in other browsers and on phones.

Will I lose my sounds or projects?

Back them up with the editor before installing or changing version. The installer can also install earlier versions, but a project saved by a newer version may not open right in an older one.

The installer doesn't find my FM-1.

Use a USB cable that carries data (many cables only charge), connect it straight to the computer without a hub, and close other programs that use the FM-1 over MIDI.

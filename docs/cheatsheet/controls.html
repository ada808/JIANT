NAVIGATION · SIX RULES

1 · SELECT WALKS THE PAGES

Every page of the section in one chain, a page a detent, stopping at both ends. No levels, no zoom.

FX: SENDS → DIST → AUTO PAN → BUSES → DLY → DLY 2 → … → PUNCH

2 · THE BUTTON: IN, OR ON

From another section: the page you were last on there. Already inside: the first page of the next group (round).

FX › DLY 2: FX → REVERB · EDIT › FILTER: EDIT → FOCUS

3 · PRESETS LOADS PRESETS

Always: the track's sounds; SEQ the patterns (PHRASES, OCT+ loads); GEN the genre. Never a page, a track or a module.

FX › DLY: PRESETS ▶ the next sound

4 · KNOB 1 PICKS

The step, the bar, the slot, the stage, the operator, the kit's sound, the row. The track: always ALGORITHM.

SEQ › STEP: K1 = all 64 steps · SEQ › BAR: K1 = the bar

5 · HOLD = THE LAYER

Held + SELECT: HOME GEN · EDIT SEQ LFO FX their dice · GLO the tempo · ENV SCL ARP SAVE their chain.

SEQ + G5 CLR STEP · SAVE + C5 NAME

6 · THE SCREEN SAYS WHERE

Tabs on top: the section's groups, the one shown lit, a dot a page. Footer: SECTION · GROUP · page and n/N.

FX · DELAY · DLY 2 6/16

THE PANEL

KNOBS AND TRANSPORT

MASTERMaster volume.
SELECTWalks the section's pages, a page a detent, stopping at the ends (the MENU: every row). Held with a button: HOME GEN; EDIT · SEQ · LFO · FX their dice (OK keeps, CANCEL back); GLO the tempo; ENV · SCL · ARP · SAVE their chain.
PRESETSAlways presets: the sounds (factory and user) on every page; SEQ: the patterns (PHRASES); GEN: the genre. Nothing in the MENU or with a button held.
ALGORITHMSelects the track from any page: T1–T4, or T1–T6 with TRACKS 6. MENU: the tabs. A layer held: MAP · KNOBS · STATE.
KNOB 1–4The four columns on screen; KNOB 1 picks (step, bar, slot, row). Fast sweeps the whole range, slow fine-tunes. With a layer held: the layer's.
OCT− · OCT+The keyboard's octave (both together: back to 0); on DRUM the kit stays put: +1/+2/+3 ratchet x2/x4/x8, −1 SHORT, −2 GHOST, −3 FLAM, each sound its own (grid KNOB 3 HIT). On action pages and dialogs: back / confirm.
PLAYStart / stop. GLO + PLAY: restart from the top.
RECArms live recording on the selected track (notes and knob moves, overdub).
HOMETap: HOME (on HOME the next group: ECO · BEING · GEN). Hold: the MENU.
KEYS27 keys play the selected track in its scale; with a layer held they become that layer's.

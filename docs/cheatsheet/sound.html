DICE

EDIT + SELECT: a random factory preset of the engine, every parameter moved up to a quarter of its range. DRUM-X: both sides of every sound around the factory kit. Heard at once: OCT+ OK keeps it, OCT- CANCEL brings the sound back. SEQ / LFO / FX + SELECT: the sequence, the LFO, the effects, the same way. HOME + SELECT (or HOME twice on HOME): GEN, the song generator, HOME's last three pages: GENRE (EXPLORE, where it opens: no genre, every rule broken; RANDOM; 43 genres; PRESETS too) · SEED · ENERGY · FORM; DRUMS · BASS · CHORDS · ARP · LEAD amounts; COMPLEX · SWING · HUMAN (SELECT: the next page). OCT+ makes the piece (again: a new seed), OCT- back to ECO. GLO + G4 undoes.

MACROS M1–M4

Hold LFO: the knobs are M1–M4, latched until LFO again. Each session starts with 2 random routes a track; F3 rolls new ones, G3 clears. By hand: MOD, SRC M1…M4. Saved with the project.

SEQUENCER

64 steps a track, up to 4 notes a step, piano roll and drum grid. One chain, five groups: PATTERN · TRACK · BAR (BAR: K1 the bar; STEP: the roll / grid, K1 all 64 steps; NOTE) · STEP (COMP: one step large, its chips) · AUTO; SELECT walks, SEQ jumps to the next group, PRESETS loads a pattern. Hold a step + turn a knob: a parameter lock. Step recording: hold a chord + KNOB 1 its length; + ARP: arpeggiated (K1 rate, K2 order, K3 oct). COMP / COMP 2: two components a step (CHANCE, RATCH, ARP NOTES / SEQ / SCALE, TRIG, RAND, OCT, TONAL, BEND, ECHO, PULSE, JUMP, REV, FILL, KILL, WALK, CLIMB, TOUCH: a kit's SHORT / GHOST / FLAM); a TRIG or FILL wins; WALK / CLIMB: a degree / an octave further every N loops (K4), UP DOWN UP-DN RND over a range (K3), stackable; ARP: ORDER × range, RATE in note values (x1 1/32 1/32T 1/64 1/64T 1/128); RATCH / ECHO: DIV; EVERY (KNOB 4): the loops the step's components play on (ALWAYS 1:2 2:2 1:3 .. 4:4 1:8 FIRST !FIRST SKIP). TRACK: PITCH, CHANCE thins the whole track, HUMAN one amount (velocity, timing, gate, decay / release), REC Q. STRETCH / REPEAT act as the knob turns; ROTATE reads the sequence from another step; NOTE's MICRO moves a step off the grid in 64ths (±32/64; REC Q 0 % records where you played, to the 64th). REC + knobs while playing: automation (64 records a section, MOTION page plays / clears them, AUTO LIST edits them).

SCALE · ARP

ROOT and SCALE are every melodic track's: CHR MAJ MIN DOR MIX PEN MPEN HARM PHRY LYD LOC MEL BLUES WHOLE DIMHW DIMWH. QNT snaps keys or the sequence, ALL TR (SCL KNOB 4) moves every sequence along the scale. CHORD: a key plays a chord (TRIAD 7TH ADD9 SUS2 SUS4 in key, MAJ MIN 7 MAJ7 MIN7 POWER), VOICING LEAD leads each to the next, STRUM as a harp.

SONGS

8 projects × 4 sections (A–D), a song of up to 16 rows by bars (SAVE › PROJECT › ARRANGE, OCT+ adds a row, PLAY plays; END: STOP, x2–x8, LOOP), SLOOP style. Each row can hold a scene: mutes, DRUM-X group mutes, macros and MIDI punch-ins. SAVE held plays sections live on the next bar, stores them, records the song (SONG REC). Each section keeps its sounds, steps, motion, kit and its SLICER.

MASTER

CLIPPNCHDUCKREL

CLIP saturates without getting louder. PNCH drives the drums: denser body. DUCK: the kick pulls the rest down (REL its release). An always-on leveler (slow 2:1, −9…+6 dB) before the limiter keeps patches at a steady volume.

TAPEWOW--

After MASTER: TAPE saturates, darkens the highs and lifts the lows; WOW the wow and flutter (par9: much stronger at 100).
